Before you try this

This is an early prototype, not a finished product. A few things worth knowing:

  • The worksheet content is AI-generated. Please check any maths answers or facts before giving it to a child — don't assume it's correct.
  • Please don't upload or paste a worksheet containing a child's full name or other identifying details.
  • Nothing you enter is stored on the server beyond generating your result.
